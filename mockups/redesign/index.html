Three ways to cut the clutter

Same data, same engine. The difference is what a first-time visitor sees before they scroll, and how much of the tool is on screen at once.

A — Answer first

The cost is the page. Inputs collapse to three quiet cards; analysis appears once, below.

Recommended

B — Three steps

One question at a time: system → usage → result. Kindest to newcomers, slowest for regulars.

C — Workbench

Everything visible, strictly zoned: section rail, work area, sticky results rail.

In all three: the instructions, the 3-step tutorial, the duplicate “all systems” summary and the empty donut are gone; there is one copy of each number; and the page opens on a real answer instead of “0 devices”.
