ⓘ About & Methodology

How we calculate

Per-component power at a workload is computed as:

P(w) = P_idle + (P_tdp − P_idle) × util(w)

PSU efficiency is interpolated from the 80+ curve for the rating tier and load percentage. Wall draw = component sum ÷ efficiency. Tiered (slab) tariffs are walked in order; taxes are applied per the location's rules.

Data sources

  • Component specs — manufacturer pages, TechPowerUp database
  • Electricity tariffs — public utility regulators (CEA, EIA, Ofgem, Eurostat)
  • FX rates — exchangerate.host (free, no key)
  • Geolocation — ipapi.co (free tier)
  • Benchmarks — TechPowerUp, Gamers Nexus, Blender Open Data, PassMark, WhatToMine, Puget Systems

Customisation

Every component has a per-system TDP override (e.g., for overclocked CPUs, third-party coolers). Per-system custom peripherals (fans, RGB, USB hubs) and per-system custom workloads (utilization profile) are supported.

Disclaimer

Numbers are estimates. Your actual electricity bill is the ground truth. Tariffs change frequently — always verify with your utility.

Contribute

PRs welcome on github.com/amitwh/pc-power-calculator. Especially: new countries / states / components / benchmarks.
